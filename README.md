## 🚀 Featured projects

### 🏛️ [Budget Watch PH](https://github.com/MJ-Dev-SE/PUBLICITY-KNOWS)
Civic-education app that maps the Philippine 2026 national budget (₱6.793 trillion, RA 12314) — the projects it funds, the agencies behind them, and which ones show no progress on the ground. Interactive Leaflet map, computed project statuses, and an AI assistant grounded strictly in the curated dataset.

`React 19` · `TypeScript` · `Vite` · `Tailwind v4` · `Leaflet` · `Recharts` · `Groq`

**[Live demo](https://publicity-knows.vercel.app)**

### 🎯 [EasyJobAIStatus](https://github.com/MJ-Dev-SE/EASYJOBASTATUS)
Smart job-application tracker and AI job-hunting assistant. Track applications through a full status timeline, score your resume against a job description, research companies, and generate follow-up messages.

`React 18` · `TypeScript` · `Supabase` · `Google Gemini` · `Tailwind` · `Recharts`
